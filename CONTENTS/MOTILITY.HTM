Welcome to the Oculomotor Functions & Neurology CD-ROM*.

The material presented in this CD-ROM is intended primarily for Optometry (O.D.) students enrolled in an Ocular Motility / Oculomotor Neurology course.  Text presented in this CD-ROM has been almost exclusively adapted from course notes written by Prof. Clifton M. Schor, O.D., Ph.D., University of California at Berkeley School of Optometry. Video has adapted from Dr. Robert D. Yee, M.D., Indiana University School of Medicine.
